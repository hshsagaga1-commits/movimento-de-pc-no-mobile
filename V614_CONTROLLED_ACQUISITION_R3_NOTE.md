# V614 Controlled Acquisition R3 — predeclared acquisition revision

This revision asks whether the V614 Touch-versus-relay result survives temporal and initial-pose control. PC Legacy remains the external target; relay is only an experimental route and is not native PC mouse input. No camera, movement, gain, or causal mechanism is changed.

## Evidence available before R3

The official September 23 Essential R2 run had 32 eligible Touch and 32 eligible relay segments. The unchanged baseline matched 16, while Moderate had seven candidate edges connecting five Touch and three relay segments, hence maximum one-to-one cardinality three. Of all 1,024 potential edges, the sequential existing Moderate gates left 536 after direction, 327 after yaw, 153 after net pitch, 151 after absolute pitch, 45 after duration, 21 after local Head translation, 18 after rotation, and seven after circular animation phase. Among the 151 pitch-compatible edges, 106 failed duration. Durations: Touch median 0.1330 s (range 0.0498–0.1998), relay median 0.2499 s (range 0.1499–0.3998); frames: 7 versus 15 median. Pitch and direction also reduce the common pool. These are candidate counts, not independent sample sizes. The reported `controlledRejectionCounts` records only the *first* failed gate for each candidate.

## Predeclared acquisition, applied identically to both routes

1. Keep ABBA, real Touch, V604 ownership/relay and the standing gate. Before A1, capture one read-only neutral reference while no camera/joystick finger is down and a valid dominant animation has phase `0.50 ± 0.02` of its cycle. This reference is chosen before either input route, without using Head-horizontal outcomes. It stores animation identity and Humanoid state, FOV/viewport, local `PrimaryPart:ToObjectSpace(Head.CFrame)`, camera-to-primary rotation and Head depth. If any reading is unavailable, wait without arming; allow emergency shutdown.
2. At the beginning of each new ~60° segment, use the already captured *before-update* snapshot. Arm only if dominant track/state and camera configuration match the neutral reference and cycle phase is `0.50 ± 0.045`. The phase band ensures a maximum cross-route initial phase gap of 0.09 cycles, below the unchanged Moderate 0.10 caliper. Also require distance to the neutral reference ≤0.025 stud for local Head translation, ≤1.25° for local Head rotation, ≤2.5° for camera-to-primary rotation, and ≤0.025 stud for initial Head depth. These are half the Moderate pairwise calipers and apply to both routes. No acquired segment is retroactively selected by Head displacement or an observed effect.
3. Keep existing segment formation and its pitch/yaw/duration validity unchanged. Guidance targets a *nominal* ~60° traversal in 0.18–0.22 s, the preobserved intersection of plausible Touch and relay durations; it only says whether the observed pace is faster/slower, without writing input or rejecting a segment for pace. Live Moderate potential uses precisely the fixed Moderate candidate gates and maximum-cardinality stage; it never selects or promotes a result.
4. Freeze each of A1/B1/B2/A2 at exactly **80 eligible segments**, regardless of live Moderate count, p value or direction of effect (160 segments per route). The old 3/32 controlled yield would project 15 at 160 per route, a margin of three over the 12-pair requirement. This projection is not a guarantee: correlations, graph overlap, and the neutral pre-arm gate can defeat it. Do not collect dynamically until 12 or until significance. Every rejected arm/segment and the fixed phase totals remain visible in the report.

The original Baseline matcher, Moderate/Strict/Broad calipers, max-cardinality/minimum-cost solver, bootstrap, FULL↔ESSENTIAL parity and causal fail-closed decision remain unchanged. If Moderate still yields fewer than 12, no V615, Astra or PC-equivalence claim follows. If it reaches 12, Touch/relay still needs a separate mechanistic connection to real PC Legacy before any implementation target is identified.

## Validation and mobile limits

The UI reads cached Baseline coverage rather than rerunning its matcher on each visual refresh. It recomputes potential Moderate cardinality only when the number of eligible segments changes; this is read-only guidance and never an early stop. Tests execute the pure arming gate in Luau, verify fixed 79/80 transition independently of pair count, pin the original Baseline and other protected source slices, and compare FULL↔ESSENTIAL decisions and transport. No desktop test can establish that 80 eligible segments or 12 Moderate pairs will be obtained on the iPhone. The exported arm-rejection counts and per-window completion flags make a failed attempt auditable.
